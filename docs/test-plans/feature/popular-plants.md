# Test plan: feature/popular-plants

## Summary

Every plant page shows **Popular right now**: up to four other plants, most units sold in the last 30 days first, each with “{units sold} sold this month”. The plant on the page is left out. Out-of-stock plants are left out. `GET /api/popular` returns that ranking (`unitsSold`, default 4, limit 1–12). At 20 shoppers at once, 95% of those answers and 95% of `GET /api/plants` come back within 300 ms, and no request fails. Expectations below are the documented ones: a failure against the app is a possible finding, not a reason to change the assertion. `web/src/components/PopularList.tsx`, its CSS, `web/src/slots/plant-page/popular.tsx`, `api/src/features/popular.ts`, and `docs/features/popular-plants.md` are covered by these rows. Nothing in `package.json` or config changed, so this plan does not schedule a full-suite run. This plan is not approved. Rows Q1–Q5 stay unresolved (`assumed — ask`) and get no test until a human resolves them. No document publishes the sales ledger, so a specific `unitsSold` figure is not asserted.
